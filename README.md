## Martin Ritchie

AI engineer. I take language models from research to production.

I have spent ten years in applied machine learning, seven of them fine-tuning and shipping language models. Before that I did a PhD in applied mathematics, on random networks and epidemics on graphs. Both still show up in what I build.

### What I'm working on

**[craeft](https://github.com/martinritchie/craeft)**: random graphs with controlled structure, for stochastic simulation and GNN benchmarking. It grew out of my PhD work.

**[teacher](https://github.com/martinritchie/teacher)**: turns ML papers into podcast-style audio. An agent parses the PDF, an LLM writes the script, a TTS model reads it. I listen while driving.

**Rust, on the way to GPU programming.** A hand-written [BPE tokenizer](https://github.com/martinritchie/bpe), checked against Hugging Face's tokenizers, and a [starling murmuration](https://github.com/martinritchie/electro-magnetic-starlings) simulation built with macroquad.

### Earlier work

In 2019 I fine-tuned GPT-2 for multi-agent simulation, steering stance and content through control tokens. In 2021 I built a ViT-to-GPT-2 classifier with trie-constrained decoding over an ontology of 10,000+ labels. More recently I built the LLM integration layer behind a content and translation platform at Go City.

The research code from my PhD is here too: [network generation algorithms](https://github.com/martinritchie/Network-generation-algorithms) from my Journal of Complex Networks paper, and [PGF-ODEs](https://github.com/martinritchie/PGF-ODEs) for SIR epidemics.
